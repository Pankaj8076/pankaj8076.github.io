About Me
Not Your Typical Analyst

I am a Financial & Data Analyst based in Pune with a background that most people in finance don't have — and most people in data don't have either. That intersection is where I operate.

With a BE in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (CGPA 8.94) from Savitribai Phule Pune University, I was trained to think in systems, find signal in noise, and solve problems with precision. That foundation shapes everything I do with financial data.

After graduation, I spent 2.5 years preparing for UPSC Civil Services and Indian Armed Forces examinations — clearing AFCAT and CDS written exams and attending two SSBs. This wasn't time away from a career. It was an intensive education in Indian economics, public policy, geopolitics, defense strategy, and world history that most finance professionals never get.

Since February 2024, I have been working as an Analyst at Sakon — a US-based global technology expense management firm serving Fortune 500 clients — doing financial reconciliation, cost analysis, variance investigation, and enterprise client management. I was recognised with a Certificate of Appreciation for 100% SLA adherence and independently leading a full data cleanup initiative.

I actively use AI tools in my daily workflow — from generating complex Excel formulas and accelerating report generation, to drafting client communications and building dashboards. I see AI as a force multiplier for financial analysis, and I'm building my career at the intersection of finance domain expertise and AI-augmented productivity.
